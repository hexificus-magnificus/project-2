Implementation notes:

Several systems require accessing multiple components from the same
entity. Because components do not provide the EntityID they are
associated with, these systems are unable to use the optimization of
iterating through components rather than entities.
Affected systems: CameraSystem, CollisionLateSystem, CollisionSystem,
GravitySystem, InputSystem, MovementSystem, SpawnSystem, SpriteSystem

In our InputSystem, dashing can only happen if the player is already in motion. 

In SpawnSystem, 10 stars are spawned and have a random initial velocity.
